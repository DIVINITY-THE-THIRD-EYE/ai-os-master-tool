## 2. EU AI Act Risk Categorization & Rules

All AI OS v4 workflows and agents are categorized into risk tiers under the EU AI Act:

| Risk Tier | Definition / Scope | Mandated System Controls |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Unacceptable Risk** | Social scoring, subliminal manipulation | **STRICTLY PROHIBITED** (Auto-blocked by PDP) |
| **High Risk** | HR/Hiring, Healthcare, Infrastructure, Legal | Mandatory HITL Approval, Full Audit Trail, Risk Impact Assessment |
| **Specific Risk** | Chatbots, Synthetic Media, Code Generation | Clear Transparency Disclosures, Watermarking |
| **Minimal Risk** | Internal optimization, test generation | Standard Logging & Security Policies |
