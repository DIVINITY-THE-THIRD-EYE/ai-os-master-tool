## 2. Risk Evaluation Matrix (Scoring Rules)

- **Probability Scale**: 1 (Very Low) to 5 (Very High)
- **Impact Scale**: 1 (Insignificant) to 5 (Catastrophic)
- **Risk Score**: `Probability x Impact`
  - **Low (1 - 6)**: Acceptable risk, monitor periodically.
  - **Medium (8 - 12)**: Active management and defined contingency plan required.
  - **High (15 - 25)**: Urgent executive escalation and active mitigation required.
