## 3. Crisis & Incident Communication Protocol

In the event of a critical project delay or production outage:
1. **Immediate Notification**: Inform Executive Sponsor via phone call within 30 minutes.
2. **War Room Channel**: Post status updates every 30 minutes in `#incident-war-room`.
3. **Public Status Page**: Update `https://status.{{DOMAIN}}` within 15 minutes of verified incident.
