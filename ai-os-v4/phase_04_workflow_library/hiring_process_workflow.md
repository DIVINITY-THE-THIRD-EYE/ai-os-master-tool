# Hiring Process Workflow Specification

## 1. Purpose & Objective
Orchestrate job specification creation, candidate sourcing, initial screening, technical evaluation, interview coordination, and offer drafting.

## 2. Prerequisites & Trigger Conditions
- **Prerequisites**: Approved headcount budget, role competency matrix, compensation band guidelines.
- **Trigger Conditions**: Department headcount requisition approval.

## 3. Participating Agent Roles & Responsibilities
- **HR Specialist**: Manages ATS job postings, candidate screening calls, interview scheduling, and offer letters.
- **Technical Interviewer**: Conducts technical coding/architecture interviews and scores candidate competency.
- **Hiring Manager**: Defines role requirements, conducts culture fit interviews, and makes final hiring decisions.

## 4. Step-by-Step Execution Sequence

### Step 1: Role Specification & Job Posting
- **Inputs**: Headcount requisition, department competency matrix, compensation band.
- **Actions**: Draft job description (JD), define mandatory vs preferred qualifications, post to ATS (Greenhouse/Lever) and job boards.
- **Outputs**: Published Job Description & ATS Requisition.
- **Verification**: Hiring Manager sign-off on JD requirements.

### Step 2: Candidate Sourcing & Resume Screening
- **Inputs**: ATS applicant pool, LinkedIn Recruiter database.
- **Actions**: Screen inbound applications against mandatory criteria, execute outbound sourcing, select candidate pool for phone screen.
- **Outputs**: Screened Candidate Shortlist.
- **Verification**: HR Specialist confirmation of candidate qualifications.

### Step 3: Recruiter Phone Screen & Intake
- **Inputs**: Candidate shortlist, screening question rubric.
- **Actions**: Conduct 30-minute phone screen evaluating candidate background, salary expectations, remote status, and availability.
- **Outputs**: Candidate Intake Assessment Notes.
- **Verification**: Candidate alignment on compensation range and start date.

### Step 4: Technical Evaluation & Panel Interview
- **Inputs**: Qualified candidate pool, technical interview scoring rubric.
- **Actions**: Conduct 60-minute technical assessment / architecture interview; score candidate across technical criteria.
- **Outputs**: Technical Evaluation Scorecard.
- **Verification**: Technical Interviewer formal recommendation (Hire / No Hire).

### Step 5: Final Interview & Offer Execution
- **Inputs**: Top candidate technical scorecard, executive approval.
- **Actions**: Conduct final Hiring Manager interview, run reference checks, draft formal offer letter, secure candidate signature.
- **Outputs**: Executed Offer Letter & Onboarding Handoff Packet.
- **Verification**: Signed candidate offer letter.

## 5. Decision Gates & Branching Rules
- Gate 1: Recruiter screen must verify salary expectation alignment before scheduling technical panel.
- Gate 2: Minimum 2 positive interview scorecards required prior to extending job offer.

## 6. Failure Modes & Fallback/Recovery Procedures
- Failure Mode 1: High candidate drop-out rate during interview process -> Action: Shorten interview stage turnaround times to < 48 hours.
- Failure Mode 2: Candidate rejects offer -> Action: Conduct exit feedback, re-engage runner-up candidate.

## 7. Artifact Delivery & Output Standard
Published Job Description, ATS Candidate Scorecards, Reference Check Notes, and Signed Offer Letter.
