# User Research Workflow Specification

## 1. Purpose & Objective
Design user research scripts, recruit participant panels, execute usability test sessions, synthesize research themes, and update personas.

## 2. Prerequisites & Trigger Conditions
- **Prerequisites**: Research hypothesis, prototype / wireframes, target user demographic criteria.
- **Trigger Conditions**: New product ideation phase or major UI redesign initiative.

## 3. Participating Agent Roles & Responsibilities
- **UX Researcher**: Designs research protocols, conducts interviews/usability tests, and synthesizes findings.
- **Product Manager**: Translates research insights into feature backlog items and product strategy.
- **UI Designer**: Observes usability sessions, updates wireframes and UI prototypes based on feedback.

## 4. Step-by-Step Execution Sequence

### Step 1: Research Plan & Interview Protocol Design
- **Inputs**: Research objective statement, target user profile specs.
- **Actions**: Formulate research hypotheses, design interview scripts, draft usability task scenarios, select testing tools.
- **Outputs**: User Research Plan & Script Protocol.
- **Verification**: Product Manager approval of research plan.

### Step 2: Participant Recruitment & Screening
- **Inputs**: Target user profile, recruitment screener survey.
- **Actions**: Screen candidates, schedule 8-12 qualified research participants matching persona demographics.
- **Outputs**: Participant Schedule & Screening Log.
- **Verification**: Target quorum of verified participants recruited.

### Step 3: Usability Test Session Execution
- **Inputs**: Interactive Figma prototype, user session recorder (UserTesting / Lookback).
- **Actions**: Conduct 45-minute moderated usability sessions, observe user navigation errors, record qualitative feedback.
- **Outputs**: Recorded Session Videos & Observer Notes.
- **Verification**: 100% of scheduled usability sessions completed and recorded.

### Step 4: Qualitative Synthesis & Affinity Mapping
- **Inputs**: Session notes, Miro / Figma whiteboarding tool.
- **Actions**: Extract key observations, group into affinity mapping themes, identify major usability pain points and success rates.
- **Outputs**: Affinity Map & Usability Pain-Point Matrix.
- **Verification**: UX Researcher validation of synthesized themes.

### Step 5: Insights Report & Design Recommendations
- **Inputs**: Affinity map, prototype recommendations.
- **Actions**: Publish User Research Insights Report, present video highlights to engineering/design, update user personas.
- **Outputs**: User Research Insights Report & Persona Updates.
- **Verification**: Formal design handoff meeting with UI Designer.

## 5. Decision Gates & Branching Rules
- Gate 1: Minimum of 8 qualified participant sessions required for statistically valid qualitative insights.
- Gate 2: Usability pain points with >50% failure rate must be remediated in design before build allocation.

## 6. Failure Modes & Fallback/Recovery Procedures
- Failure Mode 1: High participant drop-out rate -> Action: Recruit 25% over-budget buffer for all participant panels.
- Failure Mode 2: Participant unable to complete prototype task -> Action: Note usability blocker, provide prompt intervention, log critical UX failure.

## 7. Artifact Delivery & Output Standard
User Research Plan Document, Usability Session Recordings, Affinity Mapping Matrix, and Research Insights Report.
