# Business Planning Workflow Specification

## 1. Purpose & Objective
Synthesize strategic market analysis, financial projections, operational resource requirements, and risk matrices into actionable business plans.

## 2. Prerequisites & Trigger Conditions
- **Prerequisites**: Executive charter, market research reports, historical financial statements, strategic objectives.
- **Trigger Conditions**: Annual strategy cycle or new business unit proposal.

## 3. Participating Agent Roles & Responsibilities
- **Strategy Consultant**: Conducts TAM/SAM/SOM analysis, competitive benchmarking, and strategic framing.
- **Business Analyst**: Maps operational capabilities, resource allocation requirements, and organizational structure.
- **Financial Analyst**: Builds 5-year financial projections, P&L models, cash flow forecasts, and sensitivity analyses.

## 4. Step-by-Step Execution Sequence

### Step 1: Market & Competitive Analysis
- **Inputs**: Industry research reports, competitor filings, customer survey data.
- **Actions**: Analyze Total Addressable Market (TAM), Serviceable Addressable Market (SAM), build Porter's Five Forces matrix.
- **Outputs**: Market Analysis & Competitive Positioning Document.
- **Verification**: Strategy Consultant validation of market sizing methodology.

### Step 2: Operational Strategy & Resource Mapping
- **Inputs**: Business goals, department headcount estimates, tech stack needs.
- **Actions**: Define organizational hiring plan, operational workflows, milestone roadmaps, and key performance indicators (KPIs).
- **Outputs**: Operational Plan & Organizational Roadmap.
- **Verification**: Business Analyst confirmation of resource feasibility.

### Step 3: Financial Modeling & Revenue Forecasting
- **Inputs**: Pricing strategy, CAC/LTV estimates, operational cost projections.
- **Actions**: Build 5-year financial model including Income Statement, Balance Sheet, Cash Flow, and sensitivity scenario analysis.
- **Outputs**: 5-Year Financial Model (Excel / Financial System).
- **Verification**: Financial Analyst sign-off on unit economics and cash runway.

### Step 4: Risk Assessment & Mitigation Matrix
- **Inputs**: Market analysis, financial model, operational dependencies.
- **Actions**: Identify regulatory, financial, operational, and competitive risks; define mitigation strategies and trigger conditions.
- **Outputs**: Risk Register & Response Plan.
- **Verification**: Executive Sponsor sign-off on risk mitigation framework.

### Step 5: Executive Business Plan Compilation
- **Inputs**: All section deliverables (Market, Operations, Financials, Risks).
- **Actions**: Synthesize findings into an Executive Business Plan document and Board Presentation deck.
- **Outputs**: Master Business Plan Document & Pitch Deck.
- **Verification**: Formal Board / Executive Committee approval.

## 5. Decision Gates & Branching Rules
- Gate 1: Financial model unit economics (LTV/CAC >= 3.0) must be validated prior to plan compilation.
- Gate 2: Executive Board approval required for resource allocation and budget release.

## 6. Failure Modes & Fallback/Recovery Procedures
- Failure Mode 1: Financial projections show cash negative within 12 months -> Action: Revise pricing structure, reduce OPEX headcount growth.
- Failure Mode 2: Competitive threat invalidates core differentiator -> Action: Pivot value proposition, update TAM analysis.

## 7. Artifact Delivery & Output Standard
Master Business Plan PDF, 5-Year Financial Model spreadsheet, Risk Register, and Board Presentation Deck.
